
✅ TOPIC 1: Mastering Power Hitting in Tennis Ball Cricket

Slide 1 — Title

MASTERING POWER HITTING
Tennis Ball Cricket Edition

Slide 2 — The Core Principle

Power comes from timing + bat speed, not raw strength.
The lighter tennis ball rewards precision more than force.

Slide 3 — Technical Breakdown

• Stable stance
• High backlift
• Controlled downswing
• Wrist acceleration at impact
• Hip rotation for momentum

Slide 4 — Key Drills

• Wall batting reflex drill
• High-toss timing drill
• Swing-speed ladder drill
• Target hitting with markers

Slide 5 — Takeaway

Consistent big hitting = discipline, timing, biomechanics.
Refine the science, and boundaries become routine.

Slide 1 — Title

BOWLING VARIATIONS THAT WIN T10 MATCHES

Slide 2 — Essential Variations

• Off-cutter
• Leg-cutter
• Slower ball
• Taped-ball swing
• Skid length ball

Slide 3 — Mechanics

Cutters → finger roll
Slower balls → deeper grip
Swing → tape on one side
Skids → higher arm release

Slide 4 — Drills

• Cone-based length targeting
• One-step power delivery
• Wrist-position isolation
• Taped-ball swing practice

Slide 5 — Takeaway

A bowler with 3–4 reliable variations becomes a match-changer.

✅ TOPIC 4: Equipment & Gear Guide

Slide 1 — Title

THE RIGHT GEAR FOR BETTER TENNIS BALL CRICKET PERFORMANCE

Slide 2 — Bats

Look for:
• 900–1050g weight
• High sweet spot
• Thick edges
• Strong grip texture

Slide 3 — Balls

• Hard tennis ball = pace + bounce
• Soft tennis ball = control + grip
• Taped ball = swing + skid

Slide 4 — Footwear

Choose:
• Rubber soles
• Shock absorption
• Ankle stability
• Firm heel support

Slide 5 — Takeaway

Correct gear improves power, control & safety.
