Dream League of India (DLI) is a premier tennis ball cricket league organised by Servotech Sports, designed to empower grassroots talents and create a pathway for local players to compete at a professional level.

All amateur cricketers who meet age and eligibility criteria set in the DLI rulebook are welcome. Typically, players from 16 years and above can register through the official registration portal.

Visit the Registration page and complete the online form with your personal and sports details. Confirm by selecting your trial center and paying the participation fees (if applicable).

The minimum age is typically 16 years; refer to the latest announcements for updated eligibility details.

Team count varies each edition. In the latest edition, over 50 teams participated from across the country.

Register online, choose the nearest trial location, and attend on the scheduled trial dates with the required documents. Keep an eye on trial updates via the official site and social channels.

Stay Vigilant Against Scams

It has come to our notice that certain Fraudsters are impersonating Dream League of India representatives, tricking people into paying for DLI IDs and registrations through emails, calls, and messages.

Dream League of India has never called for any DLI IDs/Personal Details/Financial Details. Do not share information with unauthorized people or platforms.
